> *"True mastery emerges not from accumulation, but from intentional constraint."*

Welcome to the **Exocortex Garden**.

This is a personal, static digital garden exploring how to use local language models as focused thinking partners for brainstorming, mapping trade-offs, and structured reflection—without surrendering human agency, delegating critical judgment, or relying on opaque cloud tooling.

* **Zero Bloat:** Pure, fast static hypertext. No tracking, no cookies, no behavioral profiling.
* **Local-First:** Exploring ideas with models running directly on your own machine, anchored in your own plain-text notes.
* **Grounded Thinking:** Applying systems thinking, clear constraints, and mindful craft to software, writing, and conceptual design.
